Candy Sales Analysis Dashboard

 Project Overview
This project analyzes candy sales data using Power BI to understand sales performance, product profitability, regional trends, sales target achievement, and factory-related product performance.

 Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and gross profit.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Compare actual sales with sales targets across divisions.
- Explore sales performance by region and geographic location.
- Examine sales and gross profit associated with products linked to factories.

 Tools Used
- Power BI — data modeling, DAX measures, and interactive dashboard development.
- Excel — data inspection and preparation.

 Key Business Questions
1. How do sales change over time?
2. Which products generate the highest sales and gross profit?
3. Which regions generate the most sales?
4. Which divisions meet or exceed their sales targets?
5. Where are the candy factories located?
6. Which factories are associated with the highest-selling products?

] Dashboard Features
- KPI cards for sales, gross profit, orders, and units sold.
- Monthly sales trend analysis.
- Ranked product performance charts.
- Actual-versus-target comparison by division.
- Geographic sales and factory location maps.
- Product performance tables.
- Interactive slicers for date, division, city, and region.

Files
- `Candy_Sales_Dashboard.pbix` — interactive Power BI report.
- `screenshots/` — dashboard preview images.


 Limitations
Factory-related results represent the sales and gross profit associated with linked products.
They do not measure production efficiency because production volume, capacity, and factory operating costs are not available.
